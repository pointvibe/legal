PointVibe — SMS Text Messaging Consent

This page documents how end users consent to receive SMS text messages from PointVibe (PointVibe Inc), for A2P 10DLC verification.

Where consent is collected

Customers provide consent on PointVibe web forms: when they book an appointment or join a loyalty program at a business that uses PointVibe, they enter their phone number and check the consent box below before submitting. The same consent step is shown on the live booking and loyalty-join forms.

The consent step (as shown to the user)

Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Program details

  • Sender / brand: PointVibe (PointVibe Inc)
  • Message types: transactional — staff invitations, booking confirmations, appointment reminders, verification/OTP codes, loyalty pass recovery codes, waitlist offers. No marketing.
  • Opt-out: reply STOP at any time. Help: reply HELP or contact contact@pointvibe.com.
  • Data: we do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
