PointVibe — SMS Text Messaging Consent

This page documents how end users opt in to SMS text messages from PointVibe (PointVibe Inc), for A2P 10DLC verification.

Where consent is collected

Customers opt in on two PointVibe web forms that businesses using PointVibe share with their customers:

  • Appointment booking — book.pointvibe.com/<business>, on the "Your details" step.
  • Loyalty program join — join.pointvibe.com, on the join form.

The customer enters their mobile number and may check the SMS consent box. The box is unchecked by default and is optional: the booking or membership is completed whether or not it is checked, and no SMS is sent to a customer who leaves it unchecked. SMS consent is its own checkbox and is never combined with agreement to the Terms of Service or with any marketing consent.

The consent checkbox (as shown to the user)

Optional

On the loyalty-join form the same checkbox reads "Text me about my membership and account (pass recovery codes, verification codes and account notifications)", followed by the same disclosures and links.

Program details

  • Sender / brand: PointVibe (PointVibe Inc)
  • Message types: transactional only — booking confirmations, appointment reminders, one-time verification codes, loyalty pass recovery codes, waitlist offers, account notifications, and staff invitations. No marketing or promotional messages.
  • Frequency: varies based on the customer's bookings and account activity.
  • Opt-out: reply STOP at any time. Help: reply HELP or contact contact@pointvibe.com.
  • Data: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
